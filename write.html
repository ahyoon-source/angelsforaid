The Angel Times is a place to learn something new. Pick an issue you know well that most people haven’t heard much about, and teach it to us. Go narrow: instead of feminism as a whole, how sexism is shaping life for people in one country or region. Selected pieces are published on our Substack and sent out in The Angel Times.

  • Stick to one place, one group of people, one issue. The more specific, the more we learn.
  • Assume we’ve never heard of it. Explain what’s happening, who it affects, and why it matters.
  • Tell us where you stand. We want your informed opinion, not just the facts.
  • Any length is welcome. Most pieces are 500–1,500 words.
  • Paste straight from Google Docs or Notes. We’ll handle the formatting.
